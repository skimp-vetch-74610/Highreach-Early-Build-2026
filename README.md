## 🕹️ About

Highreach is an open-world survival action RPG set across a shattered continent of floating islands altered by ancient magic. Build and expand settlements, rescue survivors, craft equipment, fight through procedural dangers, and explore the skies aboard a customizable airship. This Windows build is an early, unfinished slice that surfaced ahead of schedule.

For players who want a rough-edged open-world survival RPG built around exploration, settlement management, crafting, combat, and co-op.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🛩️ **Airship Base** — Build, upgrade, and use your airship as a mobile base between floating islands.
- 🏕️ **Settlement Building** — Construct and manage an expanding encampment for rescued survivors.
- 🧭 **Open-World Exploration** — Explore a procedurally generated world filled with biomes, landmarks, dungeons, and secrets.
- ⚔️ **Flexible Combat** — Switch between melee, ranged, magic, defensive moves, and specialized skills.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Survivor Stories** — Rescue companions, assign them professions, and follow their personal quests.
- 🔧 **Crafting and Survival** — Gather resources, craft gear, and manage hunger, thirst, temperature, health, and stamina.
- 🌦️ **Living World** — Adapt to changing weather, day-night cycles, environmental threats, and shifting conditions.
- 👥 **Co-op Play** — Play solo or team up with up to five players across the shattered world.
